# Every template, rendered

All 120 sentence frames: 20 training and 4 held-out per attribute, each rendered twice against one fixed value -- once with a **female pronoun** and once with a **male name** (`Alan Turing`, deliberately not one of the 2,500 generated people). The value is **bold**.

**What to check.** Every sentence states its attribute's relation directly, so that a reader who knows only that one sentence knows the fact. Pronoun and name renderings are both grammatical. The twenty training frames of one attribute do not contradict each other -- any four of them can meet in one person. Nothing in a frame gives the value away.

Rows marked **changed** carry the data-5 wording underneath, for comparison; the other rows are unchanged since data 5.
